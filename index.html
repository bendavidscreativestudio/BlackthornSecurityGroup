Who we are

Blackthorn Security Group is a developing South African private security business. We are building a service around clear communication, lawful practice and practical protection for businesses and property owners.

Our core principles

Professionalism

Courteous, disciplined conduct at every post.

Vigilance

Attention to detail and awareness of risk.

Accountability

Clear records, clear responsibility, honest reporting.

Responsible Protection

Lawful, proportionate and respectful security.

Security services

Guarding Services

Uniformed guards who maintain a visible, disciplined presence and record incidents accurately.

Access Control

Gate and entrance control, including visitor and vehicle logging, to keep entry points orderly.

Perimeter Monitoring and Patrols

Scheduled patrols and perimeter checks designed around the layout and risks of your property.

Commercial and Retail Security

Security presence suited to trading hours, customer flow and stock protection.

Industrial and Warehouse Security

Site security for storage and operational facilities, with attention to deliveries and after-hours periods.

Event Security

Crowd-aware security support for events, subject to the relevant authorisation, trained personnel and availability.
